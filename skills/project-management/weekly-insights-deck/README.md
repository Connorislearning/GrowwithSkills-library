# Weekly insights deck

Turns a week of numbers into eight slides a client reads in two minutes.

## The problem

Weekly reports eat a morning and still get skimmed. Most are screenshots of Ads Manager with a few notes. The reader has to figure out what happened.

## My process

Every deck has the same eight slides. Headline. KPIs. Pacing. What worked. What did not. Next week. Decisions needed. The reader always knows where to look.

Every slide title is the takeaway. "CPA fell 18% after the hook refresh" instead of "Performance." If someone reads only the titles, they still get the story.

The deck pulls from my other skills. Pacing comes from the QoQ pacing skill. Problems come from the daily spend check. Creative findings come from the video analysis. So the report is a byproduct of work I already do, not a separate job.

It never invents a number. If data is missing, the slide says so.

## What it gives you

A Google Slides deck, built straight in Drive when the connector is available, or a .pptx when it is not.
